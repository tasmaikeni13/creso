# Frozen Phase 02 protocol — CRESO r002

The JSON file is executable. Freeze all four implementation files and this
protocol before development; commit them before final draws. Development selects
one of 24 configurations per method **per law** using 256 independent contexts.
Final evaluation uses 4,096 untouched contexts per law, with paired common random
numbers. No optional stopping, exclusions or additional configuration selection.
Seeds 42–44 are reserved for future training and are not used.

Each context is a one-step matrix problem with curvature 1 and operator radius
0.5. Population gradient and noise law are known to the evaluator, never to the
optimizer. Eight replicas freeze the four candidate vertices and noise subspace.
The next 512 supply 256 difference pairs; all 640 post-proposal replicas supply
the validation mean. Calibration and validation statistics may be dependent:
the proof unions a calibration failure with errors exceeding **true** moment
thresholds. It does not condition a mean bound on a random estimated envelope.
Every peer may average **all 648** replicas. A further 256 independent replicas
assess realized progress and are inaccessible to selection or methods.

Primary response is exact population quadratic progress, computable because
these are constructed laws. Independent noisy assessment is a secondary check.
There are nine primary paired contrasts: three laws by ideal Muon, published
NS5 in float64 with explicit operator feasibility repair, and hard spectral
projection. Bonferroni-adjusted two-sided Student intervals must have lower
endpoints above 0.01. These Monte Carlo intervals are asymptotic, not the
algorithm's nonasymptotic certificate. Report the empirical distribution,
MCSE, effect size and paired bootstrap sensitivity. A separate prespecified
mixed-signal contrast tests validation against fixed deflation at level 0.05;
its lower endpoint must exceed 0.01. This is a distinct family and is not pooled
with the primary discovery claim. Keep all 12 laws and all controls.

Gates: exact segment verifier and actual matrix feasibility pass; CRESO is active
in at least 90% of each primary law; all nine primary effects and the mixed-law
mechanism effect pass; on each of the ten Gaussian iid laws a simultaneous
Clopper–Pearson upper bound on certificate failure is below the declared 0.05.
The two invalid-assumption laws are diagnostics, never evidence of guaranteed
coverage. No threshold can be weakened after viewing final results.

Additional controls: soft spectral shrinkage, radial SGD, first-step AdamW,
spectral powers, deflation using the full mean, TONGA-style sketch inversion,
the original exact RASP update, uncertified interpolation, validation reused
from proposal data, zero and a full-mean direction with CRESO's Frobenius norm.
TONGA/deflation may tie CRESO in the rank-one orthogonal laws: those gains are
not evidence that validation or generic aggregation is novel. Optimizer maps
are initial-state/local analogues; no Shampoo/SOAP/MONA training result exists.
The eligible registry remains an obligation for Phases 03–07, not a claim that
this local study compares complete trajectories for every registered peer.

All directions are feasible; returned CRESO certificates are for curvature 1.
Only rank and upward allowance inflation are tuned for CRESO. Peers tune six
shape/regularization choices by four scales, with radial repair after scaling;
deflation/uncertified controls tune four ranks by six scales. Equal trial count
does not imply equal compute. Record host time, decomposition counts, oracle
budget, state and communication. Do not interpret CPU time as TPU throughput.

Failure: retain the untouched final run and mark failed gates; a changed method,
law, primary comparison or protocol needs a new revision and fresh final draws.
