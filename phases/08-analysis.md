# Phase 08 — Statistics and claim audit

**State: not started.** Depends on Phase 07. Start only when requested.

Analyze the frozen primary metric using seeds as independent experimental units.
Checkpoints, layers and tokens are repeated measurements, not extra seeds.
Use prescribed paired contrasts, multiplicity correction and practical effect
thresholds; distinguish superiority, equivalence and insufficient precision.
No extrapolation from three seeds to universal dominance is allowed.

Report uncertainty, failures, all eligible peers, losses/ties, time and memory,
certificate assumptions and numerical reliability. Examine whether validation
rather than known shrinkage accounts for gains. Compare against norm-matched
and covariance controls. Use independent replication where the protocol allows.

Reconcile each claim with exact Lean domain, source-reported evidence and measured
training results. Novelty is a bounded literature inference; extreme novelty or
a breakthrough needs a substantial demonstrated advance over strongest prior work.
Gates: complete statistics, exact provenance, negative evidence and no omitted peer.
